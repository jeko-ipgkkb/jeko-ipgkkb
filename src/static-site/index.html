About JEKO

MORE THAN
A CLUB.

JEKO — Kelab Jasmani dan Kokurikulum — is the official sports and co-curricular club of IPG Kampus Kota Bharu. We exist to build educators who understand the body as deeply as they understand the mind.

From physical education and competitive sport to leadership development, teamwork and community service, JEKO shapes well-rounded student teachers who lead by example — on the field, in the classroom and in the community.

JEKO At A Glance

Vision & Mission

PURPOSE IN MOTION

01

VISION

To be the leading sports and co-curricular community of IPG Kampus Kota Bharu — cultivating physically literate, disciplined and inspiring educators who move with purpose and lead with passion.

02

MISSION

  • 01Promote physical education, fitness and a lifelong culture of sport.
  • 02Develop leadership, teamwork and integrity through competitive and recreational activity.
  • 03Strengthen unity and discipline across every faculty and committee.
  • 04Serve the wider community through sports outreach and co-curricular initiatives.
